## Come to a meeting

- When: Fridays at 6:00 PM during the semester
- Where: NPB 1.226 on the main campus
- Bring: a laptop if you have one, otherwise just yourself

There is no application and no prerequisite. Show up, introduce yourself,
and an officer will walk you through how we are organized.

## Where to find us

We are on Discord, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Twitch, all linked in
the menu above and in the footer.

Announcements go out on Discord first. Photos and short video land on
Instagram and LinkedIn, and longer write ups come out of the research
groups as they make progress.

## How to reach us

- Discord is the fastest way to reach an officer, and the best place to
  ask about joining
- RowdyLink is our official student organization profile, and is where
  events get posted
- For anything else, ask an officer on Discord and we will point you at
  the right person

## Office hours

Officers are around before and after general meetings, and on Discord
most evenings during the semester.
