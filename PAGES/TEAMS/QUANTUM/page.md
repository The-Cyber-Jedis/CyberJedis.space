## About

Our research group studies cryptography at and beyond the quantum
boundary. The work splits into two directions: the protocols that
quantum computing puts at risk today, and the lattice-based and other
families being standardized to replace them.

## Mission

Quantum computing is close enough that the cryptography protecting most
of the traffic on the internet today is being actively discussed as
temporary. We want students to understand which protocols are already
affected, what the replacements are, and how far the standardization
effort has actually gotten.

## What We Do

- Work through quantum key distribution protocols, starting with BB84
- Track the standardization work on post-quantum replacements
- Run hands-on sessions and case studies on the results
