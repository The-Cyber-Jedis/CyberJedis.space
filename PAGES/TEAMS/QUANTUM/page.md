## About

Our research group studies cryptography at and beyond the quantum
boundary. The work splits into two directions: the protocols quantum
computing puts at risk today, and the lattice based and other families
being standardised to replace them.

## What We Do

- Work through quantum key distribution protocols, starting with BB84
- Track the standardisation work on post-quantum replacements
- Run hands-on sessions and case studies on the results
