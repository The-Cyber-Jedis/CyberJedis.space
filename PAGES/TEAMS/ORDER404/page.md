Order404 is our creative team. We run cybersecurity events built around
controlled chaos, and we are the group working on growing the
organization's presence.

## About

Order404 started as a place for people who wanted to do cybersecurity
that did not follow the usual script. What it has become is a project team
where members design and run events, then document them.

## What we do

- Design and run cybersecurity events
- Film and edit the video that comes out of them
- Handle the announcements and posts that go with each event
