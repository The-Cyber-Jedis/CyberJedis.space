## How we are organized

We are split into research groups and project teams. A research group
picks a subfield, writes its own training plan, and works through it
together. A project team builds something specific, like a competition
entry or a media production.

Everyone belongs to at least one group, and nobody has to pick just one.
If you are new, come to a general meeting and sit in on a couple of
sessions before you decide.

Research groups:

- **Cyber Warfare and National Security**, covering offensive security,
  malware analysis, and digital forensics in the context of modern
  conflict
- **Quantum Cryptography**, covering quantum key distribution and the
  post-quantum replacements being standardized for them

Project teams:

- **Order404**, our creative team, running cybersecurity events around
  controlled chaos
