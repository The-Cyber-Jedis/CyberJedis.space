# Teams

> Every team and research group in the organization.

## How we are organized

A research group picks a subfield, writes its own training plan, and works
through it together. A project team builds something specific, like an
event or a piece of media.

Everyone belongs to at least one group, and nobody has to pick just one.
Research groups meet on their own schedule, which you will find on their
page.

## Joining a group

Come to a general meeting, then sit in on a couple of sessions to see what
interests you. There is no application and no prior experience needed.
