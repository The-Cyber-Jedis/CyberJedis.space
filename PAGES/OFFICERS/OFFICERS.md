# Officers

> The students who keep the organization running.

## Who does what

Officer positions open up every semester. Roles are grouped into
divisions below, and every officer has their own page with their role and
how to reach them.

If you want to take on a division, help run a research group, or work on a
project, come to a general meeting or reach out on Discord. No prior
experience is required for any of it.
