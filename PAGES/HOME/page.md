## About our mission

We are a student run organization at the University of Texas at San
Antonio focused on hands-on cybersecurity. We split the work across
research groups, competitive teams, and a media team, so that anyone can
find a place to start whether they are new to the field or already
running their own labs.

Everything we run is open to members. Meetings, trainings, and events are
listed on the events page, and every team keeps its own page.
