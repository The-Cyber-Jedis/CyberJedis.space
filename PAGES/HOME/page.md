## About our mission

We are a student run organization at the University of Texas at San
Antonio focused on hands-on cybersecurity. Our work is split across
research groups that go deep on a subfield and a project team that puts on
events, so there is a place to start whether you are new to security or
already running your own labs.

Everything we run is open to members. General meetings, trainings, and
events are open to anyone who shows up, and no prior experience is
expected.
